Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Who we are

SWK Properties ('we', 'us') is a real estate brokerage at Al Saqr Business Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai, UAE. You can reach us at info@swkpropertiesuae.com or +971 56 433 0448.

What we collect

Information you give us through our contact form, newsletter sign-up, email, phone or WhatsApp: your name, email address, phone number, enquiry details and property preferences.

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. The contact page embeds a Google map, which may load content from Google when displayed.

Why we use it

To respond to your enquiry, arrange viewings, prepare shortlists and mortgage or legal introductions you ask for, and, if you opt in, to send market updates. We rely on your consent and on our legitimate interest in running our business.

Who we share it with

Only with partners needed to deliver what you asked for, such as developers, banks and legal partners, and with service providers who help us run our systems. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and meet our legal and regulatory record-keeping duties. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under UAE data-protection law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Email us at info@swkpropertiesuae.com to make a request.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
