Getting Approved

What you'll need for a UAE mortgage

01

Pre-Approval

Passport, six months of bank statements, and proof of income. Takes 5-10 working days.

02

Property Valuation

The bank commissions an independent valuation once you've signed a reservation form.

03

Final Offer

Formal offer letter issued, outlining rate, tenure and conditions for your signature.

04

Mortgage Registration

0.25% registration fee paid to DLD; the mortgage is registered against the title deed.
