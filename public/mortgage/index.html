Getting approved

What you'll need for a UAE mortgage

  1. 01

    Pre-Approval

    Passport, six months of bank statements and proof of income. Typically takes 5–10 working days.

  2. 02

    Property Valuation

    The bank commissions an independent valuation once you've signed a reservation form.

  3. 03

    Final Offer

    A formal offer letter outlines rate, tenure and conditions for your signature.

  4. 04

    Mortgage Registration

    A 0.25% registration fee is paid to DLD and the mortgage is registered against the title deed.

Questions

Mortgage & buying costs FAQ

Can foreigners buy property in Dubai?

Yes. Non-UAE nationals can buy freehold property with full ownership rights in designated freehold areas. Your consultant will confirm that a specific project is in a freehold zone before you reserve.

What are the total costs of buying a property?

Beyond the price, budget for the 4% Dubai Land Department (DLD) transfer fee, a DLD administration fee (AED 4,000 for properties of AED 500,000 and above; AED 2,000 below), agency commission (commonly 2%), and for mortgaged purchases a 0.25% mortgage registration fee plus bank arrangement and valuation fees. We provide a full cost breakdown before you commit.

How do off-plan payment plans work?

Most developers offer staged plans such as 60/40 or 70/30 (percentage due during construction versus on handover), and some offer post-handover plans that extend payments after completion. Each listing page shows the specific plan.

Does buying property qualify me for a Golden Visa?

A property investment of AED 2 million or more can qualify you for a 10-year renewable Golden Visa, subject to current immigration rules. Conditions have changed over time, so we check eligibility for your specific purchase before you buy.

What financing is available to overseas buyers?

Non-residents can typically borrow up to about 50% of the property value, while UAE residents can typically borrow up to about 80% on a first property, subject to bank policy. We compare several banks for you.

What ongoing costs should I expect as an owner?

Annual service charges (billed per square foot for community and building maintenance) and, if you let the property, a property management fee, typically a percentage of annual rent. Note that the 5% municipality housing fee on rented homes is normally paid by the tenant through their DEWA bill.

How is the estimated yield on your listings calculated?

Where we show a yield, it is an estimated gross figure: annual rent for comparable units in the same building or community divided by the purchase price. It is not net of service charges, fees or vacancy. We show yields only for ready properties, where rents can be benchmarked. Our advisors can prepare a tailored net projection.

Can SWK Properties manage my property after I buy it?

Yes. Our property management team handles tenant sourcing, Ejari registration, rent collection, maintenance coordination and renewals.
