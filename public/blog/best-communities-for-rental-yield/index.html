Where Dubai Rental Yields Tend to Run Highest — and How to Check

By the SWK Properties team · 4 May 2026 · 1 min read

Rental yield is the annual rent divided by the purchase price. Communities with lower entry prices relative to achievable rents tend to show higher yields, which is why Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC) and Business Bay are often cited by investors.

Gross versus net

A gross yield ignores costs. Net yield subtracts annual service charges, property management fees (commonly a percentage of annual rent), maintenance and vacancy between tenancies. Always ask which one a quoted figure is.

What drives demand

Proximity to business districts and transport, building quality, the supply of similar units and the mix of tenants all move rents. A community that looks strong today can soften if a large amount of new supply completes nearby.

How to test a yield claim

Compare the quoted rent with recent registered tenancy contracts (Ejari) for similar units in the same building, then deduct service charges and a vacancy allowance. Treat any projection as an estimate, not a guarantee, and ask your consultant to show the working.
