Five Dubai Communities With the Strongest Rental Yields Right Now

4 May 2026 · 5 min read

Gross rental yields across Dubai currently range from 5% in ultra-prime waterfront addresses to over 8% in high-density, amenity-rich communities. Jumeirah Village Circle leads the pack thanks to affordable entry prices and consistent tenant demand from young professionals, while Business Bay benefits from proximity to Downtown at a materially lower price point per square foot. Dubai Hills Estate and Dubai South are emerging as yield plays as new retail and metro infrastructure lands.
