The Complete Guide to Dubai's Golden Visa Through Property Investment

By the SWK Properties team · 18 May 2026 · 1 min read

The UAE Golden Visa grants a renewable 10-year residency to eligible property investors, with eligibility that can extend to a spouse and children. The headline requirement is a property investment of at least AED 2 million.

Key points

The property can generally be financed, subject to the bank's and the authorities' conditions on the equity you hold. Investment may be spread across more than one property in some cases. Whether off-plan purchases qualify depends on the current rules and the stage of the project, so confirm this before you buy rather than after.

The process in outline

Once the purchase is registered with the Dubai Land Department, you obtain the title deed and supporting documents, then apply for the visa through the relevant authority. Processing times vary with the authority's workload and your documents.

Check the current rules

Golden Visa criteria have been updated several times. Treat this article as a starting point and confirm thresholds and conditions with our legal partners or the official channels before you commit funds. Contact us and we will check eligibility for the specific property you are considering.

This article is general information, not financial, legal or investment advice. Market conditions and regulations change; speak to a consultant before making a decision.
