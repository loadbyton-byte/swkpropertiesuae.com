Our story

Built by agents, run for clients

SWK Properties was started in 2016 by a small team of agents frustrated with how opaque property buying could feel for outsiders. Today we are a full-service brokerage with 40+ consultants, and the founding principle hasn't changed: every fee, payment-plan clause and yield figure gets explained in plain language before you sign anything.

We work across off-plan and secondary sales, mortgage arrangement and long-term property management, so the relationship doesn't end at handover.
